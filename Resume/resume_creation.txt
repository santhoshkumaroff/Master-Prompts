PHASE 2 - REWRITE AND GENERATE FILES

KEYWORD STRATEGY
- Target 100% of Tier 1 and at least 80% of Tier 2 keywords that I can truthfully claim.
- Placement by weight: Skills section, Summary, first 5 words of bullets, project tech-stack lines.

CONTENT SELECTION (when my resume is longer than the page limit)
Do NOT shrink below 10pt font to fit content. Select by JD relevance instead:
1. Rate every role, project, bullet, skill and certification as High / Medium / Low relevance to the JD.
2. Keep all High items. Keep Medium items only if space remains. Drop Low items.
3. Experience: keep every role; give 3-4 bullets to the most relevant roles and 1-2 to the rest.
4. Projects: keep the 3 most JD-relevant (4 if space allows). Merge small related projects only if they truly belong together, without changing names or facts.
5. Skills: JD-matching skills first, then others by priority. Drop unrelated tools.
6. Education: keep degree, institution, dates, grade. Drop school-level details unless the JD asks.
7. Never drop: contact details, job titles, company names, dates, degrees.
8. List everything removed under "Removed for space" so I can add items back.

PAGE LENGTH
- ONE page for freshers and anyone under 3 years of experience.
- If I have 3+ years of experience or many strong relevant roles and one page forces major cuts, ask me whether I want a 2-page version before building.

CONTENT RULES
- Bullets: strong, varied action verb + what + tools/technologies + outcome (only if it exists in my resume). Maximum 2 lines each. No first person. No buzzwords such as "passionate", "hardworking", "team player".

- Projects: each states problem, tech stack, my contribution, result.

- Entry layout: bold company/project/institution on the left with dates right-aligned using a right-aligned tab stop (not a table); role or degree in italics on the next line.
- Real bullet list formatting, not typed symbols. Same indentation in every section.
- All headings, dates and bullets align to the same left and right margins.
